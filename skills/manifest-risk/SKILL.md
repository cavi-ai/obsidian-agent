# Manifest: risk register

**REQUIRED SUB-SKILL:** obsidian-agent:manifest-core

## Lens

Blockers, contradictions between notes (cite both sides), single points of failure,
unstated dependencies, stale assumptions. Score likelihood × impact and order by severity.

## Operationalizer

`obsidian-agent:plan-to-spec` for mitigations that require building.
