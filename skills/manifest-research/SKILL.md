# Manifest: research director

**REQUIRED SUB-SKILL:** claude-obsidian:manifest-core

## Lens

Coverage map: what the vault establishes and where it is thin. Open questions the notes
raise but do not answer, thinly sourced claims, unresolved contradictions, adjacent areas
absent entirely.

## Operationalizer

claude-obsidian:source-digest for evidence from existing source notes, or
claude-obsidian:vault-synthesis on the narrowed question.
